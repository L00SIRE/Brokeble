✈️ Before You Arrive
When is the AI Hackathon?
Check-in will begin on Saturday, Oct 17th at 9 AM. The hackathon will end on Sunday, Oct 18th around 4 PM.

How can I stay up to date before and during the hackathon?
Join our Discord server! The link has been emailed to accepted hackers.

Where can I see the event schedule?
See our full event schedule at live.lahacks.com/schedule.


How do I check in?
You will receive a personal QR code that will be used to scan you for food, merch, and events throughout the hackathon. To get the QR code, please visit live.lahacks.com, log in, then save it to your Apple/Google wallet. We encourage you to set up the QR code beforehand for a quick check-in experience. You will also receive it in your email prior to the hackathon.


See the check-in section below for more details.

What should I bring?
Below is our recommended packing list:

Government-issued ID (REQUIRED FOR CHECK-IN)
Phone
Laptop
Chargers / portable chargers
Headphones
Refillable water bottle (we will not be providing water bottles, there will be water fountains)
Jacket/sweater
Any medications you may need
Snacks (if you have dietary restrictions)

While we will not have a sleeping area or showers at the LA Hacks AI Hackathon, feel free to bring:

Ear plugs
Change of clothes
Toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, shampoo, towel, shower shoes)
Do I need a team beforehand?
Not at all! Find teammates by posting or looking in #team-formation on our Discord server, or check live.lahacks.com/schedule to see when in-person team formation is. Note that the maximum of team members is 4.


If you created a team during the application process, you are free to either stick with your team or find new teammates. Your team isn’t official until you submit your project.

🚗 Getting to the AI Hackathon
Where is the AI Hackathon?
Our event will take place at UCLA’s James West Alumni Center.


Address: 325 Westwood Plaza, Los Angeles, CA 90095, United States

Apple Maps link
Google Maps link
How do I get to the AI Hackathon from the airport (LAX)?
We recommend this route from UCLA Transportation that uses the Culver City R6 line from the LAX Metro Station to UCLA. Alternatively, we suggest posting in our Discord server and coordinating carpooling with others. Ubers from the airport are typically $50 or more, and may increase during rush hours.

How does travel reimbursement work?
Travel reimbursement decisions were sent alongside acceptance decisions. Hackers who were granted travel reimbursement are required to send in their ticket confirmation for flights/trains/buses, or acknowledge that they will be submitting gas receipts after the event. Reimbursement will happen shortly after the event through the provided payment information.


Note that you are required to submit a project to our hackathon to receive travel reimbursement. Email info@lahacks.com for any personal questions.

Are buses available?
No. This year, we will not be providing any transportation via bus.

Is parking available?
Paid parking is available at UCLA lots. We recommend Parking Structures 7 and 4, which are a short walk from the James West Alumni Center. Parking Structure 8 is another close alternative in case other lots fill up. UCLA Transportation provides additional information about parking costs.


Alternatively, parking in the nearby neighborhoods (Landfair Ave, Levering Ave, Midvale Ave, Kelton Ave, Veteran Ave)  is free overnight. Note that some streets are specifically blocked off Saturday/Sunday morning for street-cleaning, and UCLA students frequently occupy that parking in the neighborhoods during the night. You can increase your chances of finding a parking spot in the neighborhoods by searching during the day.


Parking reimbursement is not provided for hackers. If you park in a UCLA lot, you must pay UCLA parking fees. Parking reimbursement will be provided for judges and panelists. Codes will be sent out via email before your arrival.

🎟️ Check-In &amp; Venue
What do I need for check-in?
You will receive an “LA Hacks AI Hackathon boarding pass” with a QR code that can be added to your Apple/Google wallet. In addition, you must have your government-issued ID to check in.

How early or late can I check in?
Dedicated check-in will be open on Saturday from 9 AM - 12 PM. Afterwards, if you have not received a badge and need to check in, please notify security at the doors.

Can I leave and re-enter the venue?
Yes! However, you must keep your badge in sight at all times.


There will be a dedicated entrance and exit. You may not enter or exit through other doors. Please find a detailed map of the venue at live.lahacks.com/map.

Where can I sleep and shower?
Unfortunately, there will not be a sleeping area or showers at the LA Hacks AI Hackathon due to venue limits and a shorter event.

How can I access the Wi-Fi?
Wi-Fi information is located at live.lahacks.com.

💻 Hacking
What are the project rules?
Each team can have a maximum of 4 members.
Teams must consist exclusively of admitted hackers.
Organizers, judges, sponsors, or in any other privileged position at LA Hacks may not participate.
Students who were not accepted to the LA Hacks AI Hackathon this year may not participate.
All coding and building must occur during the hackathon.
Ideation and planning before the hackathon is allowed.
If your team is building on an existing project, you must clearly indicate which parts were built during the hackathon. Judges will only consider new functionality or features added during the hackathon.
Teams can be disqualified from the competition at the organizers’ discretion.
Reasons may include but are not limited to: breaking the competition rules, breaking the MLH Code of Conduct, or other unsporting behavior.
All hackers must participate in-person. Your entire team must be present in-person to present to judges on Sunday.
When does hacking start and end?
Hacking starts on Saturday at 12 PM and ends on Sunday at 12 PM.


Unlike our flagship LA Hacks event, there will not be additional time to finalize your Devpost submissions, so please get your submission in before Sunday at 12 PM!

Will there be mentors?
Yes! There will be mentors available throughout the hackathon, mainly Saturday 1-8 PM. To find a mentor, head to the #mentor-info channel on Discord and open a ticket. Bot instructions can also be found in this channel.


Please wait patiently for one of our mentors to respond! You can also send a message in #find-a-mentor to converse with mentors, including finding which row your team is in.

Will hardware be provided?
Yes! Some of our sponsors will be providing hardware for hackers to use during the event.


However, availability may be limited, and we will not be providing equipment such as 3D printers or other fabrication tools. If your project requires specific hardware, we recommend bringing your own.

🏆 Tracks, Challenges, &amp; Prizes
What tracks and company challenges can I submit to?
Please check live.lahacks.com/tracks and the Devpost for the most updated information about our awesome tracks and company challenges!

How do tracks and challenges work?
Every project must submit to exactly one of the three main LA Hacks tracks. You can also submit your project to the Agentverse track sponsored by Fetch.ai and as many company challenges as you’d like, as long as your project meets their requirements.

What are the prizes?
Please check the Devpost for the most updated information about our amazing prizes!

Can I win multiple prizes?
Yes! Projects are eligible for overall prizes (1st, 2nd, 3rd place) and prizes within their selected LA Hacks track. Overall winners will not also receive a main track prize. Projects can additionally win prizes from Agentverse and company challenges, even if they've already won an LA Hacks prize.

🚀 Submitting Your Project
How do I submit my project?
In order to be judged, your project must be submitted through our hackathon’s Devpost: https://la-ai-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/

When are submissions due?
Submissions are due on Sunday at 12 PM sharp.


No code may be written after 12 PM. Additionally, unlike our flagship LA Hacks event, there will NOT be additional time to finalize your Devpost submissions, so please get your project in before the deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.

What must be included in the submission?
Please check the Devpost (“What to Submit” section) for the most updated information about what must be included in your project submission.

🧑‍⚖️ Judging
How does judging work?
Judging occurs in 2 rounds on Sunday morning. Your entire team must be present in-person.

Round 1: Science Fair Judging (12:30 - 2 PM)
Each project will be assigned a table, communicated via the #announcements channel on Discord.


Judges will visit your team's table and review your project for 4-5 minutes each. You should expect multiple judges to stop by your project. Find an organizer if you need help!

Round 2: Top 5 Demos (2 - 3 PM)
The top 5 projects (track-agnostic / regardless of track) will advance to the final round and present their project live, on stage, in front of judges and hackers. Each team will have 8 minutes total to present (3 min pitch/demo + 5 min Q&amp;A from judges).

What criteria are judges using?
Projects are evaluated on the following criteria:

Technical Depth
Are you tackling a hard problem with advanced or ambitious technical work?
Does the solution involve strong systems thinking, optimization, or custom-built components?
Product Thinking &amp; Impact
Is the problem sharp and clearly differentiated?
Does the solution feel viable in the real world with meaningful impact?
Originality &amp; Insight
Is there a genuinely novel idea, framing, or insight?
Does it stand out in a memorable way from other hackathon projects?
Execution &amp; Polish
Is the project highly polished and complete?
Does the demo feel confident and like a real product?
📃 Additional Policies
What if I am under 18?
If you are under 18 by the date of the hackathon, you are unfortunately not allowed to participate at the LA Hacks AI Hackathon. Please look forward to our events in future years!


The exception is if you will be 17 years old AND enrolled in university by the date of the hackathon. In that case, if you are accepted, please have a parent or guardian sign your waivers.

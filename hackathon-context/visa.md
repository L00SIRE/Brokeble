**The Future of Shopping: Powered by AI, Secured by Visa**

**(1st Place: $2,000)**



---



Shopping is evolving from searching and browsing to conversing and collaborating with AI. Consumers expect AI-powered experiences that help them discover products, compare options, personalize recommendations, manage budgets, and complete purchases seamlessly. At the same time, merchants need trusted, secure, and intelligent payment experiences that drive conversion and customer satisfaction.



Teams should demonstrate how AI can transform one or more stages of the shopping journey:

- Discovery

- Decision-making

- Personalization

- Checkout

- Payments

- Loyalty and rewards

- Post-purchase experiences



Judging Criteria:



Innovation (25%) – How creative does the solution reimagine the shopping experience?

User Experience (25%) – Does the experience feel intuitive, useful, and seamless?

Technical Execution (20%) - How effectively has the team implemented AI and supporting technologies?

Commerce Impact (15%) - Can the solution improve customer engagement, conversion, loyalty, or merchant outcomes?

Trust &amp; Responsibility (15%) - Does the solution address privacy, transparency, security, and responsible use of AI?
